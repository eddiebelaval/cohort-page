The certification

The Claude Certified Architect is Anthropic's official technical certification for people building with Claude. It's scenario-based, covers five domains, and is recognized across the Claude partner ecosystem. The exam and all prep coursework are free.

  • 25%Agentic Architecture
  • 20%Tool Design and MCP
  • 20%Claude Code Workflows
  • 20%Prompt Engineering
  • 15%Context and Reliability
Format: Scenario-based multiple choice
Passing: 720 / 1000
Cost: Free
Valid for: 6 months from date of passing
Issued by: Anthropic
