Cohort Playbook

Your ops guide for running the Claude Certified Architect cohort. Templates, cadence, key moments. Reference the cohort page dashboard for live data.

Weekly Rhythm

Monday -- Kickoff

Post a weekly pulse in the WhatsApp group. Pull numbers from the dashboard.

Week [N] check-in [X] members in the cohort [Y] active this week Top streaks: [names from Consistency section] This week's focus: [CPN course / cert prep / exam prep] Who's working on what? Drop a quick update.

Check the stat bar + consistency section on the dashboard for numbers.

Wednesday -- Nudge

Short mid-week energy. Tag anyone who completed something.

Mid-week update [Name] just completed [Course] -- nice work. Who else is making moves this week? Even 15 minutes counts.

Check the "This Week" celebrations section on the dashboard.

Friday -- Recap

End of week. Share progress. Build anticipation.

Week [N] recap Completions this week: [list from celebrations] Team streak: [X] days [X] of us on the CPN learning path Next week: [what's coming] Have a good weekend. See you Monday.

Do this once. Sets the tone for the group.

Quick intros -- drop your name, what you do, and why you joined. I'll start: I'm Eddie, founder of id8Labs. I build AI products with Claude. I started this cohort because I want the people I know to get certified before everyone else catches on. This credential is going to age well. Your turn.
